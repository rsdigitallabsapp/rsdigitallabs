Our story

One studio.
One product at a time.

RS Digital Labs started with a simple frustration: most apps do too much and none of it particularly well. We set out to build differently — one focused product, designed around a single clear use case, executed with care.

Our first app, ARISE, came from a personal morning practice. Every affirmation app felt bloated or performative. We wanted something that took ten seconds, worked on both platforms, and got out of the way. So we built it.

We're a small studio based in the Philippines, building for a global audience. We don't have a roadmap that stretches five years into the future. We have apps we're proud of, users we actually talk to, and a commitment to keep improving what we ship.

01

One problem per app

Feature creep is the enemy of usefulness. Every RS Digital Labs app solves one specific problem and earns its place in your life.

02

Both platforms, always

Everything we build ships on iOS and Android simultaneously. Your platform choice shouldn't determine which apps you get to use.

03

Earn attention, don't demand it

We design for ten-second interactions, not engagement loops. If an app needs to manipulate you to be used, it hasn't earned the right to exist.

What we believe

Design principles we don't compromise on.

Simplicity is a feature

Everything in an app that isn't helping you accomplish your goal is working against it. We remove features, not add them, until what remains is clear.

Quality before scale

We'd rather have 1,000 people who love an app than 100,000 who tolerate it. Growth follows quality — not the other way around.

Your data, your device

We collect as little data as possible and protect what we do collect. You should never have to wonder what's happening with your information.

Small team, real accountability

When something breaks or you have a question, you're talking to the people who built it. No ticket queues. No chatbots. Real answers.

Ship and improve

A good app that exists is better than a perfect app that doesn't. We ship early, listen carefully, and improve continuously.

Beautiful because it matters

How something looks is how it feels to use. Good design isn't decoration — it's the thing that makes a tool feel effortless instead of effortful.
