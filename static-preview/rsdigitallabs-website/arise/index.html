How it works

Three steps. Ten seconds.
Every morning.

ARISE removes every excuse. There's nothing to set up, nothing to track, nothing to configure. Just you and one intention.

01

Unlock your phone

ARISE knows when you pick up your phone for the first time each morning. No alarm needed — your own routine is the trigger.

02

Read your affirmation

A single, carefully chosen statement greets you. Tap the button, say it aloud. Three times. The voice matters — it's yours.

03

Begin your day

That's it. Ten seconds. Your streak grows, your intention is set, and you carry that statement into everything that follows.

Why ARISE

What you say in the first minute matters most.

The first thing your brain hears each morning shapes how you interpret the day. Most of us fill that moment with notifications, news, and noise that has nothing to do with us.

ARISE interrupts that pattern with one small act: your own words, spoken back to yourself. That's it. The research on intention-setting, positive self-talk, and habit formation all point to the same conclusion — mornings are the leverage point.

Intentional, not automatic

You choose your categories. ARISE adapts your affirmations to what actually matters to you right now.

Built for streaks, not guilt

The streak exists to celebrate consistency, not punish breaks. Premium users can shield their streak on rough mornings.

Ten seconds, not ten minutes

No journaling. No meditation timer. No guided content to sit through. ARISE respects your morning.
