Getting started
1
Open ARISE. You'll be guided through a short setup: choose the affirmation categories that speak to you.
2
Grant microphone and notification permissions when prompted. Both can be revoked later in Settings.
3
That's it. Tomorrow morning, ARISE will be waiting for you when you unlock your phone.
Open the app, go to Settings, then tap "Edit Categories." Select any combination of categories. Your affirmations will update starting the next day.
ARISE listens for you to say your affirmation aloud — that's the practice. Speech recognition confirms you've said it three times. Your voice is never recorded or sent anywhere; recognition happens entirely on your device. You can deny the permission and tap manually instead.
ARISE sends a local morning reminder at the time you specify during setup. This notification is scheduled on your device — we don't operate a notification server, and your notification time is never sent to us. You can change your reminder time in Settings, or turn notifications off entirely in your phone's Settings app.
Premium & Subscriptions
Premium includes: unlimited custom affirmations in your own words, access to all affirmation categories, Streak Shield (protect your streak on missed days), and all future premium features at no additional charge.
iOS
Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → ARISE → Cancel Subscription. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
And
Open Google Play → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ARISE → Cancel.
All purchases are handled by Apple or Google. For iOS refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android refunds, visit your Google Play order history. We cannot process refunds directly, but we're happy to help you navigate the process — email us at hello@rsdigitallabs.com.
Install ARISE on your new phone and sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe. Open the app, go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be re-activated automatically.
ARISE Premium is currently a personal subscription and does not support Family Sharing. Each person who wants Premium features will need their own subscription.
Troubleshooting
1
Force-close the app and reopen it.
2
Check that you're on the latest version of ARISE in the App Store or Google Play.
3
Restart your phone.
4
If the issue persists, email us at hello@rsdigitallabs.com with your device model and OS version.
Streaks are stored locally on your device and cannot be restored from the cloud if the app is deleted or the data is cleared. If you believe a streak was lost due to a bug (not a missed day), email us at hello@rsdigitallabs.com and we'll investigate.
Check that ARISE has notification permission: go to your phone's Settings → Notifications → ARISE and make sure notifications are enabled. Also check that Do Not Disturb / Focus Mode isn't blocking the notification. If the issue continues, delete and re-grant notification permission inside the ARISE app via Settings → Notifications.
Make sure the microphone permission is granted (Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → ARISE). Speak clearly and at normal volume with the phone held naturally. If you're in a noisy environment, move somewhere quieter or tap the count buttons manually — the app works either way.
Some Android devices restrict apps from launching on unlock. Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → ARISE → Battery → and make sure "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions" is selected. On some manufacturers (Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo), you may also need to disable battery optimization specifically for ARISE. Contact us if you're unsure what setting applies to your device.
